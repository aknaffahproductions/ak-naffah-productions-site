Al Naffah

Founder of AK Naffah Productions.

Stories driven by contradiction, obsession and the gap between who people are and who they want to be.

AK Naffah Productions is an independent film production company focused on emotionally driven cinema, documentary storytelling, and character-led films that explore art, identity, obsession, memory, and human connection.

Founded by writer-director and producer Al Naffah, the company develops projects that combine cinematic craft with intellectual and emotional depth — films that are visually expressive, psychologically truthful, and culturally resonant.

Current productions include Firefly: The Emily Remler Story, a feature documentary created in cooperation with Emily Remler's estate, exploring the life, artistry, and legacy of the groundbreaking jazz guitarist through rare archival material, contemporary interviews, and an immersive, music-driven approach to storytelling.
