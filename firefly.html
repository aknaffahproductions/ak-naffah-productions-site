About the project

A feature documentary exploring the life, music and legacy of jazz guitarist Emily Remler through archive material, performances and the voices of those who knew her work best.

The life of jazz virtuoso guitarist Emily Remler, as told in her own words. Produced in co-operation with Berklee College of Music, Musicians Institute and the Emily Remler Estate.

Firefly: The Emily Remler Story is fiscally sponsored by IDA (International Documentary Association), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit arts organization.

Through our fiscal sponsorship, supporters in the United States can make charitable contributions to IDA designated in support of Firefly: The Emily Remler Story. Funds raised through IDA are used to support the production and completion of the film.
